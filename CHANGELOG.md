# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-11

- Groups several apps into one stack of the Dock that opens in macOS's own fan, and keeps working when Droppy is quit.
- An editor to create groups, pick their apps from the Mac or drag them from Finder, reorder them and preview the fan.
- Dock icon: several apps on a coloured background, one app, a picture, a folder or the stacked apps.
- Order (custom or by name), names with or without “.app”, rename, Show in Finder.
- A keyboard shortcut per group that opens its real Dock stack (needs Accessibility).
- Suggestions that build a group from suites already installed, such as Adobe, Microsoft Office or the browsers.
- A copy of the Dock's preferences is kept before every change, the ten latest in Dockfan's folder.
- English and French.
