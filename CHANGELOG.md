# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-11

- Groups several apps into one stack of the Dock that opens in the Dock's own fan, grid or list.
- An editor to create groups, pick their apps from the Mac or drag them from Finder, reorder them and preview the stack.
- Apps added, removed or reordered reach a docked stack right away, without reloading the Dock.
- Choice of opening (fan, grid, list or automatic), Dock icon (the apps piled up or a folder), order (custom or by name) and names with or without “.app”.
- Suggestions that build a group from suites already installed, such as Adobe, Microsoft Office or the browsers.
- A copy of the Dock's preferences is kept before every change, the ten latest in Dockfan's folder.
- English and French.
